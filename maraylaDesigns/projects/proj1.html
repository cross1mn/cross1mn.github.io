Collateral

Mass Influence & The Design of Persuasion

Role
Director & Designer
Timeline
12 Weeks
Tools
Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, After Effects, Figma
Deliverables
Identity, Print Materials, Web, Motion Graphics
Collateral was my senior graphic design exhibition at Central Michigan University. The exhibit explored the art of persuasion and the systems that influence how people think, feel and act. Through interactive displays and environmental graphics, we examined how the elements of design and media shape perception in everyday life. Collateral was designed as an education experience for college students, and encouraged visitors to become more visually literate and critically aware of the messages they encounter across advertising, technology, news, and culture.
Our research was centered around the understanding of how design influences perception. This included campus surveys, eye-tracking studies, historical analysis, and the examination of persuasive media. By identifying recurring design tactics and patterns of influence, the thesis for the exhibition came to life. The insights gathered helped transform complex concepts into an accessible and engaging public experience.
The development of Collateral began with defining a thesis that explored how design influences perception. Through research, the project evolved into an education exhibition that examined the design tactics used to shape opinions and behaviors. With this concept in place, I developed a visual identity that reflected the exhibition's themes. The fragmented, reconstructed aesthetic was inspired by the way people gather information from countless sources and piece together their understanding of the world. Collaboration was a key part of the process. My classmates and I utilized a shared figma workspace to organize research, brainstorm ideas, discuss strategies, and provide feedback throughout the development. The platform allowed us to continue conversations outside of class, making it easier to refine concepts and maintain alignment on ideas. As the project progressed, content was organized into a clear visitor journey throughout the gallery that balanced educational depth and engagement. The final phase focused on production and installation, transforming months of research, design, and collaboration into the exhibition experience.
