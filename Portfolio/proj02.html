POSTER SERIES

Home Decor in 2024 is a 16 page booklet exploring the typography in home decorations. Pictured on the side are five of the pages in the spread. The focus was specifically on the “Millennial” or “Mom” home decor aesthetic, which stems off the modern simplicity of the current century. I tried to make the booklet itself reflect the simplicity of the signs using only black/ white, very simple graphics, and a focus on the figure/ ground relationship of the elements.
