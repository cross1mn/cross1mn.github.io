PRINTMAKING

Throughout my printmaking course, I have engaged with various forms of print. Pictured on the side are five of the pages in the spread. The focus was specifically on the “Millennial” or “Mom” home decor aesthetic, which stems off the modern simplicity of the current century. I tried to make the booklet itself reflect the simplicity of the signs using only black/ white, very simple graphics, and a focus on the figure/ ground relationship of the elements.
